ABOUT/BIO

BIOGRAPHY

Elijah Loving, a veteran graphic designer and associate professor, is also a comic book illustrator and author.  His background includes an MFA in Illustration: Comics from the Academy of Art University and an MA in Studio: Painting from Pittsburg State University.

You could say he has been in the industry since childhood.  Much to the chagrin of his English teachers, he always preferred creating illustrations over writing essays.

Currently, he lives in small-town Iowa, where the wide landscapes and big sky country of the US Midwest lend themselves well to sparking his imagination. He lives there with his family, who also challenge the boundaries of his imagination.
