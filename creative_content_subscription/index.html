Questions and answers

Why subscribe instead of hiring a full-time designer?

A full-time designer usually costs more than $55,000 a year, plus benefits. And keeping one person busy every single week is hard when your needs go up and down.

With a subscription you can pause and pick back up whenever you like, so you only pay for design when you actually need it. That frees up money to grow your business.

Who does the design and illustration work?

Me. It's just me, Elijah, so there's no middleman and no guessing who's working on your project. We talk directly and your requests get my full attention.

If we find out along the way that you need something outside my wheelhouse, like app development or UX work, I'll help you bring in the right partner and stay on as your project lead and main contact.

How fast will I get my designs?

Most requests are done in two business days or less, Monday to Friday. Bigger or more detailed requests can take a little longer, and I'll always let you know what to expect.

Your deadlines go right into my planning, and we keep everything on your Milanote board so you can see where things stand. Reach out anytime for an estimate.

How many requests can I make?

As many as you like. Add them all to your queue. On CCS Standard I work through them one at a time. On CCS Pro I work on two at once.

Can I pause my subscription?

Yes. If you only have a request or two, you don't need to use a whole month. Once your current project is done, pause, and save the rest of your days for later.

Billing works on a 31-day cycle. So if you use 15 days and then pause, you still have 16 days waiting for you whenever you're ready.

I only have one or two jobs right now. Is that too small?

Not at all. Subscriptions are month to month. Get a month, send your request, and pause when the work is done. When something new comes up, unpause and use the days you have left.

How do I send you requests?

Once you subscribe, I'll set up your own Milanote board and shape it around how you like to work. You and your team can drop in requests, files and notes, and see what's active, what's queued and what's done.

If writing a brief isn't your thing, record a quick video with Loom or share a Google Doc. If it fits on Milanote, and nearly everything does, it works.

What programs do you use, and what files do I get?

Mostly Adobe Creative Cloud: Photoshop, Illustrator and InDesign. I also work in Corel Painter, Sketch and Figma.

You can get JPG, PNG, PDF, SVG, AI, PSD and other standard formats. Just ask if you need something specific.

What if I'm not happy with a design?

Then we keep going. The job isn't finished until you're happy with it. Tell me, show me, or send me a video on your Milanote board, and I'll refine it.

Who owns the work?

You do. Your subscription includes full copyright to the designs and illustrations I make for you. If you enjoy working together, I'd be grateful if you told a friend.

What isn't covered?

Right now I don't offer 3D modeling, animation, packaging, app design, or UX and UI design.

Do you offer refunds?

No. Because the subscription is built around real-time, hands-on work together, I don't offer refunds. You can pause or cancel anytime, so you never pay for more than you want.

Is a monthly plan right for you?

Let's find out together. Book a short call and I'll walk you through how it works, or fill out the intake form and tell me about your project.
