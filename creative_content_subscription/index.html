CREATIVE CONTENT SUBSCRIPTION

A monthly art budget, by invitation only. CCS is for clients who have already commissioned a piece from me and need new art every month: a church or nonprofit with a monthly newsletter, a small publisher, or a business with a busy campaign calendar.

Every piece is digitally hand drawn by me, Elijah. I keep CCS to 2 or 3 subscribers at a time so each one gets my full attention. Your monthly credit is spent at my regular per-artwork prices, so you never pay more than a one-off client. What you gain is a reserved place on my calendar.

How CCS works

Who can join?

CCS is invitation only. It is open to past clients who have already commissioned at least one piece and know they will need art every month. If that sounds like you, send me a note and we can talk about whether it is a good fit.

What does my monthly credit cover?

Each plan covers 3 artworks at its tier: $350 each on Standard, $600 on Professional and $900 on Premium. You can also spend the same credit on any mix of my illustration and design services, at the same prices I charge everyone.

Why subscribe instead of commissioning one piece at a time?

You get a reserved monthly slot and first place in my queue, your prices stay locked for as long as you are committed, and any unused credit rolls over for 1 month.

How do revisions work?

Two revision rounds are included on every piece. Extra rounds, rush work and expanded usage are priced the same way as my one-off work, and you will always know the cost before I start.

Is there a commitment?

Yes, a 3-month minimum, billed at the start of each month.

Do nonprofits get a discount?

Yes. 501(c)(3) organizations get 20% off any CCS plan.
