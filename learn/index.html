LEARN

Welcome! This is where I share what I've picked up about drawing, illustration and design. You'll find notes on sketching, color and light, the tools I actually use, how I designed a comic cover, and talks and videos from artists I keep learning from.

There are also my TMQ interviews with fellow artists, the live streams where my backers and I built The Last Colony poster together, step by step, and a chat I had with VoyageKC about my life and work.

Some of these posts are a few years old, so a few outside links have aged, but the lessons still hold up. If you're working on something and want a hand, tell me about your project.
