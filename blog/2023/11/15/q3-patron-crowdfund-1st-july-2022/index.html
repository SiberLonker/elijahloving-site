Teutonic Monsters Comic Cover Design

November 15, 2023

🔥 Unleash the Teutonic Monsters: A Comic Pitch with Behind-the-Scenes Cover Design! 🔥

Greetings, fellow adventurers and seekers of epic tales! 🌟✨

Embark on a journey through the pages of “The Teutonic Monsters,” a graphic novel that blends history, mythology, and the unbridled chaos of battle. 🛡️🗡️

Pitch: The Teutonic Monsters: Consul Felix of the newly formed 6th Roman Legion is entrusted with the typical Roman mission to bring the wild horde of Germanic cretins to heel. Prior to the battle, rumors are spreading like wildfire through his Company that the uncivilized horde has a secret weapon: giant blood-thirsty monsters. The highly decorated, highly civilized Roman Consul, distracted with his expectation of Senate appointment, dismisses these rumors as nothing more than old women’s gossip…until he faces the undeniable truth on the battlefield.

Comic Book Cover Design: Ever wondered about the magic behind a captivating comic cover? 🎨✨ Join us behind the scenes as we unveil the process of crafting the visual gateway to our epic tale.

  1. Concept Design: It all starts with a variety of ideas, with a focus on shape and design in the overall composition. Each element is carefully evaluated to capture the essence of “The Teutonic Monsters.”
  2. Sketching and Tight Pencil: Once the concepts are developed, the ideas are sketched out further into discernible forms and features that represent the characters of the story. Ultimately leading to the final design and type, pencil, ultimately being inked and washed.
  3. Color Palette Selection: The last step is focused on enriching the design with color and vibrance, which also has a unique part of telling the story and the adventure of the comic inside.

Why Support: By joining this journey, you’re not merely a reader but a co-creator of this epic tale. Your support fuels creating a vivid world where myth and history collide and where the line between civilized and uncivilized blurs.

Prepare yourselves for the clash of civilizations, the roar of mythical beasts, and the awakening of a Consul’s realization on the battlefield.
