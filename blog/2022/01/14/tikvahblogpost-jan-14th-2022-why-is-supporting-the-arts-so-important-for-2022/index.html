TikvahBLOGPOST Jan 14th, 2022 – Why Is Supporting The Arts So Important For 2022?

OK, I know we are already two weeks into 2022 – but seriously, where did 2021 go?

Regardless of whether we are ready or not, it’s here and efforts are well in motion that it can’t be stopped. 

For our TLC (The Last Colony) comic crew, I can’t wait to see you at the KC Comicon this coming Spring 2022 click on the banner above to reserve your tickets now.  I’m officially inviting you! Our next publication of TLC is in the works. The next step is that I need to review my scripts for books 2, 3, and 4 with my editor. Having this step helps you review the script, avoid plot line holes and avoid missing links for any important characters.  I started working on the Q2 poster for patron support, without the direct support of independent artists.

Here at Tikvah inc., we are working hard at bringing back our fan and creator favorite TMQ (TikvahMinds Quarterly) 2022 magazine. Thank you for your support in spreading the word, sharing this valuable art publication with students of all backgrounds and anyone interested in illustration.  Please keep the momentum going and building. We wouldn’t be here without you!

Have a great weekend!
