Suggested Comic Supply List – 06/04/21

June 4, 2021

First and for more a lot of the type of supplies I us may not be the right ones for you. This of course depends on your style, approach, and overall budget. Whatever you do use make sure it meets those needs first, and with that said this is a list that I have found to be what I am currently using and have found some success in, regardless cool and amazing tools can never replace good old fashioned practice and lots of studies. I should also mention here that we live in amazing times and the types of tools available to us to help out with the illustration-making process truly help to speed up the work. But I should also mention that having a keen idea and understanding of the basics is equally important.

While I am not an affiliate with Blick Arts any place you can get your hands on works for me. The only place I am affiliated with is Hej Styles. Which is one of the best digital tools I have ever used as a ruler, perspective drawing, and an all-around great group to work with.

Without further adieu here is my Comic Supply List (with links):
