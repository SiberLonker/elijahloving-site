Tell me about your project

COMICS & GRAPHIC NOVEL

Comics are my passion! I can’t imagine doing anything else. Whether it’s a poster, working on my craft, or developing a full graphic novel project – I’m all in! Illustrating specifically in this genre has always been a passion for me from a young age. Telling stories in pictorial form is how I used to get into trouble at school. There is so much depth of thought that goes into the making of comics. The type of comics I seek to create have a strong sense of theatrics that combine ink, washes, and digital painting are all my kryptonite.

WELCOME TO PROFESSOR LONKER'S LECTURE HALL – Where Art Meets Mayhem

“If it doesn’t look good, don’t use it.” – Dying in Tarkov is guaranteed, but at least my guns look functional. I’m proudly anti-meta!

Greetings, scholars of chaos! I’m Professor Lonker, your resident comic artist, design snob, and gaming enthusiast. Watch as I swap ink pens for mouse clicks and get hilariously destroyed in Escape from Tarkov, Gray Zone Warfare, and Path of Exile 2—all because I refuse to embrace the meta.

Expect me to yell at scav AI, complain about sound design, mutter about grid layouts, and occasionally drop nuggets of wisdom (mostly about how gear aesthetics are more important than performance).

Come for the art. Stay for the shenanigans. And remember: if it doesn’t look good, it’s going in the trash.
